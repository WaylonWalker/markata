Markata v0.5.0 makes a site feel more connected. Blogrolls and mentions turn links into useful context, while the terminal interface grows into a practical way to explore a site's content.

## Follow conversations across the web

Blogroll feeds can include images, pagination, entry limits, and duplicate detection. Mentions support familiar aliases and domain-style identities, with hover cards that show an avatar and short bio. Feed sitemaps improve discovery, and generated pages can participate in internal-link resolution.

## Explore content from the terminal

The interactive interface adds mouse support, searchable help, scrollable Markdown, drill-down navigation, sortable tables, and a read-only configuration view. New Steam integration and richer decorative backgrounds round out the release.
