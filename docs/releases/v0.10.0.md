Markata v0.10.0 makes a growing site easier to explore, publish, and maintain. Search gains production-ready server and navbar modes, while the reader timeline gives feeds a clearer rhythm and stronger previews.

## Help readers find their next story

The redesigned reader improves the timeline and preview hierarchy. Video media now appears in article cards, and contribution graphs adapt to narrow screens. Posts can choose their format individually, and reveal slides offer another way to present long-form ideas.

## Make Markata work better with agents

Agent selection supports Vercel-style workflows, and the bundled site skill adds analytics storytelling guidance. Improvements to CLI configuration, feed filters, heading anchors, and wikilinks make everyday authoring more predictable.

Download the [v0.10.0 release](https://github.com/WaylonWalker/markata/releases/tag/v0.10.0) or inspect the [source tag](https://github.com/WaylonWalker/markata/tree/v0.10.0). See the [current agent setup guide](/docs/guides/agent-skills/) to connect your writing workflow.
